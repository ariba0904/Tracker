Five Things I Learned from Deep Work

1
Deep work creates valuable results.
Focused, uninterrupted effort helps me learn challenging skills and produce higher-quality work.
2
Shallow work can feel productive.
Messages, notifications, and low-value tasks can crowd out meaningful work.
3
Focus improves through practice.
Concentration grows when I train regularly and resist unnecessary distractions.
4
Structure makes focus easier.
Scheduling focus blocks and preparing my workspace makes deep work repeatable.
5
Review progress and rest deliberately.
A weekly review and a clear shutdown routine help protect tomorrow’s focus.
